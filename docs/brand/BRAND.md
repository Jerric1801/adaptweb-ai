# AdaptWeb AI — Brand Quick Reference

Full guidelines deck lives at **`/brand`** on the site (`web/src/app/brand/page.tsx`). Tokens live in `web/src/app/globals.css`.

## Essence

- **Line:** The web, without the pressure.
- **Who we are:** Your honest layer on the web — strips the pressure out of every page and reshapes it for how you think.
- **Values:** Honesty · Agency · Clarity · Calm
- **Personality:** Calm Guardian · Plain-spoken Translator · Quiet Ally
- **Voice:** Calm. Factual. On your side. We show; we never shout.
- **Golden rule:** If AdaptWeb would strip it, AdaptWeb doesn't use it (no urgency, scarcity, confirmshaming or invented numbers in our own UI).

## Type

- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque SemiBold, tracking −3% to −4.5%
- **Body/UI:** Geist Regular / Medium
- **Labels:** Geist Mono, UPPERCASE, +6% tracking — data callouts only

## Logo — "The Peel"

A page whose corner lifts away to reveal the honest layer (Lime). Ink outline always; fold always accent. Min 16px mark. Clear space = peel height.

## Illustration

Hand-drawn objects, 3px round Ink stroke, flat palette fills, no gradients. Metaphors: windows, tags, lenses, hourglasses, sprouts, networks.

## UI language

28px card radius · soft shadow surfaces · "sticker" chips (2px Ink border + 3px hard shadow) · dashed Lime ghost for removed elements · rounded progress bars with hatched remainder.
