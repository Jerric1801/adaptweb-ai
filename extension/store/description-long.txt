AdaptWeb AI is a browser extension that puts you back in control of every page you visit.

🧹 STRIP DARK PATTERNS
Automatically removes fake countdown timers, false scarcity claims ("Only 2 left!"), fake social proof notifications, nagging pop-ups, pre-ticked add-ons, and confirmshaming copy — so you can make decisions without pressure.

💰 TRUE TOTAL
See the real price before you check out. AdaptWeb surfaces hidden shipping fees, service charges, and estimated tax that sites bury until the last step.

📖 THREE READING MODES
Switch between Analytical (specs & comparisons front-and-centre), Minimalist (only what you need to decide), and Visual (big imagery, scannable facts) — and your preference syncs across devices.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
AdaptWeb never reads your input fields, passwords, or personal data. It only scans visible page elements to identify patterns. Your browsing history stays on your device.

⚡ AI-POWERED ACCURACY
For sites not yet in our curated library, AdaptWeb's AI engine analyses the page structure and generates rules on the fly — getting smarter with every session.
